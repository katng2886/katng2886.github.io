About

Background and education

I'm a Data Analyst at LeadingAge in Washington, DC, where I pull, clean, and analyze data from Salesforce (Nimble AMS) and outside sources to support membership, revenue, and engagement reporting. I came up through data quality and intern roles on the same team, and before that I ran an 8,000-record database migration at George Mason's Office of University Branding.

I like the parts of this work most people skip: validating fields, chasing down duplicates, making sure the number everyone trusts is actually correct. The dashboard is the easy part. Earning the right to put a number on it isn't.
